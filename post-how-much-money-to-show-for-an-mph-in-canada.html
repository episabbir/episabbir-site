How much money do you need to show in the bank for an MPH in Canada?

A real example from the University of Saskatchewan

By Md Sabbir Ahmed · November 23, 2026 · Takes 2 minutes to read

Many Bangladeshi students, especially those who finished MBBS or BDS, want to do an MPH in Canada. The first question they ask me is: how much will it cost? Here is a real example.

The program

I'll use the University of Saskatchewan (USask) as an example. Its MPH is course-based and takes about 2 years full-time. Some students switch to the thesis option after they start. Their tuition is charged per term, so the numbers below are for the course-based MPH.

Tuition

For 2026–27, USask lists the course-based MPH tuition for international students as C$39,937. This is the price of the whole program, not one year. You pay it bit by bit as you take your courses.

Student fees

On top of tuition, you pay student fees. They cover things like health and dental insurance and a bus pass. For a full-time student on campus, they come to C$1,565.82 for 2026–27.

Living costs

To get a study permit, Canada asks you to show enough money to live on for one year. The amount depends on how many family members come with you. This is the lowest amount Canada accepts. Real costs, especially rent, can be higher.

Note: These amounts are based on Canada's minimum for living costs in a study permit application. To be on the safe side, it's better to show some extra money. Taka amounts use 1 CAD = 90 BDT. Exchange rates change every day, so convert the totals into your own currency at the current rate.

You will also pay for: university application fees, sending your documents, IELTS, your study permit (visa) fee, shopping before you leave, and your flight to Canada. Add these to your budget too.

Check before you budget

I calculated these amounts in November 2026. Tuition, fees and the living cost amounts change almost every year. Before you make a plan, check the latest numbers on the USask tuition page and on Canada's proof of funds page. The links are below.
